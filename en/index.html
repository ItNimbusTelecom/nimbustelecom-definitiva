Who we are

The technology you expect, with the close attention you miss

Nimbus Telecom is an operator and a technical services company based in Vidreres. We work locally, with our own team, and every day we help people who just want something simple: clear explanations.

We believe transparency is a forgotten value in this industry and we want it back. We don't sell what we have, we sell what you need.

  • We know the area

    We know which networks reach each zone, so we tell you what options you really have at your address.

  • Real transparency

    No small print, no surprises on the bill. We explain what you sign up for and what to expect.

  • Our own team

    ICT professionals who install, maintain and support. You talk to people, not a robot.
