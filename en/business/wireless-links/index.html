What it's for

Three situations where a wireless link is the answer

A point-to-point link is a dedicated wireless connection between two fixed points, with an antenna at each end. It behaves like a network cable, without the cable.

Linking buildings, sites or offices

Two buildings of the same company on the same industrial estate or several kilometres apart: one network, one internet connection and one phone system for everyone.

Cameras and remote installations

CCTV cameras in a field, a reservoir, a car park or a building site; access control on a distant gate; sensors in an installation with no network.

Farmhouses and areas without fibre

Bringing good internet to a farmhouse, a farm or a rural guesthouse from a nearby point that does have fibre or our Wimax network.

If what you need is home internet in a rural area, see the internet page: that's where we explain our Wimax network, which is a different thing. Home internet

How we do it
